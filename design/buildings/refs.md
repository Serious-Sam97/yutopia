## Each kind's grounds (beside the building, on its plot)

- House: a walk to the door, a driveway (if the plot is wide), a mailbox, porch steps, foundation plants.
- Main Street: built to the front line, a sidewalk to the kerb; bins and a back door at the rear.
- School: its yard and a flagpole; police: a flagpole, a parking bay; fire: an apron of concrete to the street.
- Gas: a forecourt (as today), the kiosk at the back, an air pump.
- Garage: an entry ramp at the street, a stair and lift core at a corner.
